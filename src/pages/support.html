How do I move my kits to a new phone?

On your old phone, export your kits in Settings. Then import the file in KitKeep on the new phone. Restoring a phone backup doesn't always bring kits back, so exporting is the reliable way.

Do I need an account?

No. KitKeep works without an account, and your kits stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for details.
