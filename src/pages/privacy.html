Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers the KitKeep app and this website.

Your data

KitKeep has no account, and KitKeep itself has no analytics. Your kits are kept in a database inside the app, on your phone. Apart from the technical data that ML Kit sends on Android (see Camera), nothing leaves your phone unless you do something that sends it, as described below.

Backups

Your kits are stored in a database inside the app, on your phone. If you use iCloud Backup, a computer backup, or Google backup, your phone's backup may include KitKeep's data. These backups are made by your phone's operating system. iCloud and Google backups are stored by Apple or Google under their terms; a computer backup stays on your computer. KitKeep never receives them and can't read them. You can turn backups off in your phone's settings (on iPhone, also just for KitKeep). Restoring a backup doesn't always bring kits back, so to move kits to a new phone, export them in Settings and import the file there. Uninstalling KitKeep deletes its data from your phone, but not from backups that were already made.

Export files

An export file is created only when you export your kits, and it goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Camera

KitKeep can read an expiry date from a photo you take. The text is recognized on your phone (Apple Vision on iPhone, Google's ML Kit inside the app on Android); the photo is never sent anywhere and is deleted as soon as it has been read. On Android, ML Kit sends Google technical data about its use, such as the phone model, OS version, how long recognition took and an identifier for this installation, but not the photo or its text.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your phone.

Web search

When you search the web for an item, KitKeep opens Google in the in-app browser with the item's name, so Google sees that search.

More templates

When you look for more kit templates, KitKeep downloads them from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub sees your phone's IP address. Nothing else is sent.

This website

This website has no analytics and no cookies, and it loads nothing from other sites. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which sees visitors' IP addresses.

Changes

This policy is updated whenever the way KitKeep handles data changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
